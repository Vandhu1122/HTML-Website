Assignment 15: Responsive Images with <picture> & srcset

Delivering optimized images based on viewport width, screen resolution, and art direction requirements.

1. Art Direction with <picture>

The <picture> element allows browsers to choose alternate images depending on screen size. Resize your browser window to observe how the graphic adjusts.

2. Native Browser Lazy Loading (loading="lazy")

Adding loading="lazy" defers offscreen image network requests until the user scrolls near them, speeding up initial page load times.
